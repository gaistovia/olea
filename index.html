Our Story

Rooted in one grove,
carried to one table.

OLEA began as a promise between two brothers and the grove their grandmother planted in 1962: that whatever they built would still taste like where it came from. Today the same trees supply the oil that opens every meal.

Our Mission

To make Mediterranean cooking feel inevitable again — precise, seasonal, and unafraid of simplicity at the highest level of craft.

Our Values

Provenance over decoration. Every ingredient is traceable to a farmer, a boat, or a barrel we can name.

Why Choose OLEA

A kitchen led by a two-star alumnus, a cellar built over a decade, and service that remembers how you take your coffee.

Our Promise

No dish leaves the kitchen the chef wouldn't serve his own family. That rule hasn't changed since 2011.

Two-Star Alumnus
Executive Chef

Adrian Kallis

"I don't want you to taste technique. I want you to taste the grove, the boat that morning, and nothing we added to disguise it."

Trained in Lyon and seasoned across two Michelin-starred kitchens in Paris and Copenhagen, Chef Adrian returned home to the Riviera in 2011 to build a kitchen with a single rule: everything traceable, nothing borrowed from a catalogue. His tasting menus now change with the harvest calendar, not the print schedule.
